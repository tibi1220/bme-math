\[
	z^4 - (2 - 3i) z^3 - 6i z^2 = z^2 \left( z^2 - (2 - 3i) z - 6i \right) = 0,
\]
ha $z = 0$, vagy
\[
	z
	= \frac{2 - 3i \pm \sqrt{(2 - 3i)^2 + 24i}}{2}
	= \frac{2 - 3i \pm \sqrt{(2 + 3i)^2}}{2}
	= \frac{2 - 3i \pm (2 + 3i)}{2},
\]
tehát ha $z = 2$ vagy $z = -3i$.
