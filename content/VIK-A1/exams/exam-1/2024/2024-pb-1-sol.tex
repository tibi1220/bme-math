Legyen $\varepsilon > 0$. Ekkor
\[
	\left| \frac{3n - 1}{n + 5} - 3 \right|
	= \frac{|3n - 1 - 3n - 15|}{n + 5}
	= \frac{16}{n + 5}
	< \frac{16}{n}
	< \varepsilon,
\]
ha \(n > \frac{16}{\varepsilon}\), így
\(
N(\varepsilon) = \left[ \frac{16}{\varepsilon} \right] + 1
\).
