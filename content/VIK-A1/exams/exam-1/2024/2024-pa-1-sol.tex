Legyen $\varepsilon > 0$. Ekkor
\[
	\left| \frac{2n - 1}{n + 2} - 2 \right|
	= \frac{|2n - 1 - 2n - 4|}{n + 2}
	= \frac{5}{n + 2}
	< \frac{5}{n}
	< \varepsilon,
\]
ha \(n > \frac{5}{\varepsilon}\), így
\(
N(\varepsilon) = \left[ \frac{5}{\varepsilon} \right] + 1
\).
