\[
	z^4 - (3 + 4i) z^3 + 12i z^2 = z^2 \left( z^2 - (3 + 4i) z + 12i \right) = 0,
\]
ha $z = 0$, vagy
\[
	z
	= \frac{3 + 4i \pm \sqrt{(3 + 4i)^2 - 48i}}{2}
	= \frac{3 + 4i \pm \sqrt{(3 - 4i)^2}}{2}
	= \frac{3 + 4i \pm (3 - 4i)}{2},
\]
tehát ha $z = 3$ vagy $z = 4i$.
