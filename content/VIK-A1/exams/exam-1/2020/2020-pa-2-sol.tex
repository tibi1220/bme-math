A $0 = z^4 - 16$ egyenlet megoldásai, mivel
$16 = 16 (\cos 0^\circ + i \sin 0^\circ)$, a gyökvonás képletével
\[
	z_k
	= \sqrt[4]{16} \left(
	\cos \frac{0^\circ + k \cdot 360^\circ}{4} + i \sin \frac{0^\circ + k \cdot 360^\circ}{4}
	\right)
	= 2 \left( \cos(k \cdot 90^\circ) + i \sin(k \cdot 90^\circ) \right)
	\quad (k = 0, 1, 2, 3),
\]
azaz $\pm 2$ és $\pm 2i$:
\begin{center}
	\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.6, >=stealth]
		\draw[->] (-2.8, 0) -- (2.8, 0) node[right] {$\operatorname{Re}$};
		\draw[->] (0, -2.8) -- (0, 2.8) node[above] {$\operatorname{Im}$};
		\draw[dashed] (0, 0) circle (2);
		\fill (0:2) circle (2pt) node[below right] {$2$};
		\fill (90:2) circle (2pt) node[above right] {$2i$};
		\fill (180:2) circle (2pt) node[below left] {$-2$};
		\fill (270:2) circle (2pt) node[below right] {$-2i$};
	\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

A $0 = z^5 - 27i z^2 = (z^3 - 27i) z^2$ egyenlet megoldásai
\[
	z_k
	= \sqrt[3]{27i}
	= 3 \sqrt[3]{\cos 90^\circ + i \sin 90^\circ}
	= 3\left(\cos(30^\circ + k \cdot 120^\circ) + i \sin(30^\circ + k \cdot 120^\circ)\right)
	\quad (k = 0, 1, 2),
\]
azaz
\(
z_{0, 1} = \pm \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{3}{2} i
\), $z_2 = -3i$ és $z_{3, 4} = 0$.

A gyökök a komplex számsíkon:

		\fill (150:3) circle (2pt) node[above left] {$z_1$};
		\fill (270:3) circle (2pt) node[below right] {$z_2$};
		\fill (0, 0) circle (2pt) node[below left] {$z_{3, 4}$};
	\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
